# **App Name**: CivilConnect

## Core Features:

- User Authentication: Secure user login for admins (executive members) and regular members with role-based access control.
- Payment Processing: Handle online payments for membership fees, event registration, and sponsorships.
- News Feed: Display news and announcements related to the civil engineering society.
- Forum: Enable discussions and interaction among students in a forum environment.
- Executives Page: Showcase the society's executive team with their roles and contact information.
- Analytics Dashboard: Provide admins with analytics on registrations, engagement, and payments.
- AI-Powered Content Summarizer: Uses a generative AI tool to summarize long forum posts for quick reading.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: A deep sky blue (#fe7701), evocative of engineering blueprints and clear skies, lending the site a sense of stability and progress.
- Background color: A very light grayish-blue (#ffffff), nearly white, creating a clean and professional backdrop.
- Accent color: A vibrant electric blue (#1f1a50) to highlight key interactive elements, adding a touch of modernity.
- Font pairing: 'montserrat bold' (sans-serif) for headings, providing a modern, tech-forward feel, paired with 'Inter' (sans-serif) for body text to ensure readability and a clean aesthetic.
- Use consistent and professional icons related to civil engineering topics like structures, blueprints, and tools.
- A clean, modern layout with clear sections for news, forums, and executive information. Use a grid-based system for responsiveness.
- Subtle transitions and animations to enhance user experience without being distracting, like loading animations and hover effects.
